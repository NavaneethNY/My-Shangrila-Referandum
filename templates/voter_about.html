About MSLR

MSLR (Modern Shangri-La Referendum) is a secure and transparent digital voting platform designed to allow citizens of Shangri-La to participate in democratic decision-making through referendums.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a fair, accessible, and trustworthy online voting system that ensures every eligible voter has a voice while maintaining the highest standards of security and integrity.

Key Features
  • Secure voter authentication using SCC codes
  • One-person, one-vote enforcement
  • Transparent referendum results
  • Real-time vote counting
  • Election Commission oversight
Who Can Vote?

All registered Shangri-La citizens aged 18 and above with a valid Shangri-La Citizen Code (SCC) are eligible to vote.
